      Case Study Framework

      A compact revision sheet for structuring a first-pass equity case when the clock is running. Use it to keep the answer balanced across business quality, financial quality, valuation, risks, and catalysts.

      Case framework

      First-pass analysis checklist

      Work from business model to conclusion. Do not let a strong business story replace valuation and risk judgment.

      1. Understand businessWhat does the company sell, to whom, and why now?
      2. Segment mixIdentify growth engines versus mature or cyclical lines.
      3. MoatSwitching costs, scale, IP, network effects, or distribution.
      4. GrowthSeparate reported growth, window growth, and cycle recovery.
      5. Quality of growthCheck gross margin, operating margin, FCF margin, and ROIC.
      6. Cash and capexDoes growth convert to free cash flow?
      7. Balance sheetCash, debt, net debt, and strategic flexibility.
      8. ValuationSeparate absolute multiples from peer-relative screening.
      9. RisksWhat can break the thesis?
      10. CatalystsWhat could change market perception?
      11. ConclusionConclude on quality, price, risks, and catalysts.

      30 minute plan

      Case study timebox

      Use the timebox to avoid over-reading. The goal is a defendable first view, not a perfect model.

      0 to 5 minUnderstand business and documents.
      5 to 12 minRead revenue, segments, and margins.
      12 to 18 minCash, capex, and balance sheet.
      18 to 23 minValuation and peers.
      23 to 27 minRisks and catalysts.
      27 to 30 minPrepare oral conclusion.

      5 minute oral answer

      Interview structure

      Give the interviewer a clean path through the debate. Be explicit where judgment is still required.

      1. Company overviewWhat it does and where value is created.
      2. What happenedKey financial or market development.
      3. Financial qualityGrowth, margins, cash, and balance sheet.
      4. ValuationAbsolute view and peer-relative view.
      5. RisksMain downside and what to monitor.
      6. ConclusionQuality, price, risks, catalysts. No automatic buy or sell.

      Stock pitch template

      Mobile revision prompt

      Use this as a speaking scaffold. Keep each section short and evidence-led.

      Company overview - business model and segment mix.

      Investment thesis - why the market may be wrong or underappreciating something.

      Why now - catalyst, inflection, or debate.

      Financial quality - growth, margins, cash, ROIC, balance sheet.

      Valuation - absolute multiples and relative peer context.

      Risks - what breaks the thesis.

      Catalysts - what changes perception.

      Conclusion - do not conclude only on business quality. Conclude on quality, price, risks, and catalysts.

          Use this workflow to add fresher sample data or a new company while keeping the GitHub Pages dashboard fully static. The browser only imports local JSON files; the Python companion script is run on your machine, outside the frontend.

          3. Run the generator locally with a mock or manual provider.

          This dashboard is static. To add a new company, generate or prepare a JSON locally, then import it here. No data is uploaded.
